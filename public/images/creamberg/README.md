The four dielines (`4`-`7`) render in a wider band than the rest of the page,
matching the design, and sit flush against each other - their own white margins
provide the spacing. Export them with that white space intact.

Export at roughly 2x the design sizes for sharpness. Images render at their own
natural proportions, so the export crop is what shows - no cropping is applied.

Sections, headings, and image order live in `src/data/caseStudies.js`.
